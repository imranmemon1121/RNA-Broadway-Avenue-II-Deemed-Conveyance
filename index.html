Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Deemed Conveyance and 7/12 mutation.

When a property builder/developer fails to execute regular conveyance deed within 4 months of society formation, the societies can apply to the Competent Authority (DDR) for Unilateral Deemed Conveyance under MOFA Section 11. It legally transfers full ownership of the land beneath B20, B21, B22, C23, and B24 from the builder to the societies/union.
The 7/12 Extract (Satbara Utara) is the ultimate revenue record proving legal title of the land in Maharashtra. Obtaining Deemed Conveyance alone is not enough; mutating the entry (Form 6-C) at the Talathi/Revenue office ensures the builder’s name is removed and the Society/Federation name is registered as official landowner, enabling redevelopment rights in the future.
An architect registered with the competent authority drafts an Architect Area Certificate establishing pro-rata carpet/built-up land share for each of the 5 buildings based on approved layout plans and floor space index (FSI) consumed.
Members can apply for a certified copy (True Copy) of their Index II from the Sub-Registrar’s office where the flat was originally registered. The Union committee provides full guidance to affected flat owners.
